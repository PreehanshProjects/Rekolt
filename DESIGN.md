# Rekolt — a considered table

The redesign replaces the procedural Blender produce and animated botanical illustration with a restrained food editorial direction. Large Bodoni Moda headings, smaller Archivo labels, generous space, warm ivory and deep forest green create the hierarchy. Gold is limited to quiet emphasis. There are no decorative 3D objects or continuous animations.

The supplied logo from `C:/Users/preeh/Downloads/rekolt_logo_assets` is displayed unchanged in the header and footer. Both areas use light backgrounds to retain the original green and gold. Inline SVG viewports trim transparent outer space; the source PNG is unchanged.

The opening pairs an oversized headline with a welcoming, sunlit kitchen still life of lettuce, cucumbers, citrus, strawberries and herbs. This replaces the earlier dark vegetable arrangement at the user's request. Its square composition supports desktop and mobile crops; a subtle ivory fade keeps the forest-green caption legible. An ivory catalogue offers category navigation, the original French product names and botanical labels, clear units, visible prices and selection states. Fresh pasta has a separate illustrated section. The approach section communicates the registered business and ordering process without inventing delivery promises. A forest-green order section assembles the actual list.

All original 18 lines and prices are retained: 16 produce lines and two pasta lines on request. Category links filter with JavaScript and remain anchors without it. Herb unit switching, order persistence, quantities, estimates and copying remain available. Pasta prices are excluded from the estimate. WhatsApp orders go to the owner-supplied **+230 5756 3134**, with products, quantities and the estimate prefilled. Email remains hidden until supplied.

Readability was increased at the owner's request: 16–17px body copy, stronger secondary-text contrast, clear sans-serif product names, 12–13px units and botanical labels, and larger buttons. Mobile catalogue rows stack prices above their units so product names have more space. Headline typography and the supplied logo are retained.

Both editorial images were generated using the built-in image generation tool and saved as WebP in `assets/editorial/`. Complete prompts and source paths are recorded in `assets/editorial/PROVENANCE.md`. The footer identifies the imagery as AI-created.

Responsive checks cover 320, 360, 390, 600, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 pixels. Browser checks cover selection, filtering, totals, quantity controls, pasta exclusions, clipboard output, restored orders, clearing, unavailable contact controls, asset loading and reading the catalogue without JavaScript. Desktop, tablet, mobile and populated order screenshots are in `tmp/preview/`. Reduced motion and print styles are provided.
